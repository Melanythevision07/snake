How to Play

  • Objective: Be the first player to reach or pass Tile 30.
  • Rolling: Click ROLL DICE to move your pawn along the serpentine path.
  • Ladders: Land on a ladder base to climb up to higher tiles!
  • Snakes: Land on a snake head and slide down to lower tiles!
  • Trivia Tiles: Landing on designated tiles triggers an English question based on Arce, Deiner, or Mela's topics.
    • Correct answer = Stay on your tile.
    • Wrong answer = Move back 2 steps!
